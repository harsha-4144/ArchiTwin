The project

About ArchiTwin

ArchiTwin stands for Architectural Digital Twin cum Simulator. It is a small teaching tool for first-year architecture students.

1What it is

You build a simple frame building, change its sizes and materials, and see which beams bend more than the allowed limit. Every calculation runs in your browser. The server only stores the designs you save.

It is a digital twin in the sense of a live model you can change and compare. It has no link to live data from a real building or sensor.

2What it is not

Simplified model, no live-data link. Not a structural safety check.

Known limits

  • Only sagging of beams is checked. Strength, column buckling, connections, wind, earthquake and foundations are not.
  • Each beam is a solid rectangle of one material, with an evenly spread load.
  • Slab and wall weight is not added to the beam load.
  • The daylight indicator is window area divided by floor area; it is not a daylight simulation.
  • Cost and weight are rough totals from rounded rates, not a quantity survey.

Ask a structural engineer for any real building, anything people will use, or any decision about safety, cost or permits.
